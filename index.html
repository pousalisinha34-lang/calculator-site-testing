Upload Simulation Data
Upload your GRMHD simulation output as a CSV file. The code will parse the 3D grid and field quantities automatically.
📂
Required columns in CSV file:
r — Radial coordinate grid (spherical)  |  h — Polar angle \(\theta\) grid  |  phi — Azimuthal \(\phi\) grid
rho — Mass density \(\rho\)  |  B — Magnetic field magnitude  |  pg — Gas pressure
volume_element — (Optional) Cell volume; computed as \(r^2 \sin\theta\,\Delta r\,\Delta\theta\,\Delta\phi\) if absent.
Rows must be ordered in r-major (r-outer-loop) indexing. All quantities in CGS units.

Model Parameters
Set the physical and numerical parameters for the spectrum calculation. Defaults are pre-filled.
Black Hole & Accretion
Mass accretion rate at the horizon, normalized to Eddington rate. Dimensionless.
Black hole mass. Set to 14 for a typical stellar-mass BH like GRS 1915+105.
Mean molecular weight per ion. Default: 0.5 (fully ionized H plasma).
Mean molecular weight per electron. Default: 0.5 (fully ionized H plasma).
Ion-to-electron temperature ratio. Controls the electron temperature and thus synchrotron output.
Frequency Grid
Number of logarithmically spaced frequency bins. Default: 100.
Minimum frequency for spectrum. Default: \(10^9\) Hz (radio).
Maximum frequency for spectrum. Default: \(10^{22}\) Hz (hard X-ray/gamma).
Compton & Optical Depth
Optical depth above which the spectrum thermalizes (Comptonization saturates). Default: 10.
Multiplicative factor in the Wien exponential cutoff \(e^{-h\nu/kT_e f}\). Default: 2.
Max Compton scattering orders in the optically thin region. Default: 5.
Max Compton scattering orders in the optically thick region. Default: 100.
