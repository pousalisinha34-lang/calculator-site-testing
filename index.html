⚙ Model Parameters
Black Hole & Accretion
ṁ  (ṁ/ṁEdd)
Mass accretion rate at the horizon, in units of the Eddington rate
e.g. 0.01 for sub-Eddington LMXB
MBH  (M☉)
Black hole mass in solar masses
e.g. 14 M☉ for GRS 1915+105
Plasma Composition
μi
Mean molecular weight per ion; ≈ 0.5 for fully ionized hydrogen
Default: 0.5
μe
Mean molecular weight per electron; ≈ 0.5 for pure hydrogen plasma
Default: 0.5
R = Tp / Te
Proton-to-electron temperature ratio. Higher R → cooler electrons → lower synchrotron luminosity.
e.g. 300 for MAD/SANE hot accretion flows
Frequency Grid
Nfreq
Number of log-spaced frequency bins
Default: 100
νmin (Hz)
Minimum frequency — set to radio (∼10⁹ Hz)
Default: 10⁹ Hz
νmax (Hz)
Maximum frequency — set to hard X-ray (∼10²² Hz)
Default: 10²² Hz
Compton Scattering
τmax
Max optical depth above which spectrum thermalizes
Default: 10
fcutoff
Wien cutoff factor in exp(−hν / kBTe · f)
Default: 2
Nscatt,thin
Max scatterings in optically thin regime (τ < 1)
Default: 1
Nscatt,thick
Max scatterings in optically thick regime (τ ≥ 1)
Default: 5
