Required CSV columns:
r — radial grid (code units) • h — polar angle θ grid • phi — azimuthal φ grid • rho — mass density • B — magnetic field (Gaussian CGS) • pg — gas pressure (dyn cm⁻²) • volume_element — cell volume (optional; auto-computed as r² sinθ Δr Δθ Δφ if absent).
Rows must be in r-major (r → θ → φ) order.
⚙ Model Parameters
Black Hole & Accretion
ṁ  (ṁ / ṁEdd)
Mass accretion rate at horizon in units of the Eddington rate
e.g. 0.01 for GRS 1915+105
MBH  (M☉)
Black hole mass in solar masses — sets gravitational & Eddington scales
e.g. 14 M☉ for GRS 1915+105
Plasma Composition
μi
Mean molecular weight per ion; ≈ 0.5 for fully ionized hydrogen
Default: 0.5
μe
Mean molecular weight per electron; ≈ 0.5 for pure hydrogen plasma
Default: 0.5
R = Tp / Te
Proton-to-electron temperature ratio. Higher R → cooler electrons → lower synchrotron luminosity. Typical range 1–10³ for hot accretion flows.
e.g. 300 for MAD/SANE hot accretion flows
Frequency Grid
Nfreq
Number of log-spaced frequency bins. More bins = smoother spectrum but slower.
Default: 100
νmin (Hz)
Minimum frequency — set to radio (∼10⁹ Hz) to capture self-absorbed synchrotron
Default: 10⁹ Hz
νmax (Hz)
Maximum frequency — set to ∼10²² Hz to include hard X-ray IC emission
Default: 10²² Hz
Compton Scattering
τmax
Max optical depth above which spectrum thermalizes — caps Compton y-parameter
Default: 10
fcutoff
Wien cutoff factor modifying exp(−hν / kBTe · f). Smaller = sharper cutoff.
Default: 2
Nscatt,thin
Max Compton up-scatterings in optically thin regime (τ < 1)
Default: 5
Nscatt,thick
Max Compton up-scatterings in optically thick regime (τ ≥ 1)
Default: 100
Volume Element
Volume element source
If your CSV has a volume_element column, select "Use CSV column". Otherwise it is computed as r² sinθ Δr Δθ Δφ from the grid.
